Frequently Asked Questions

What if I'm a complete beginner? +

Perfect. Most of our members are hobbyists, not master craftsmen. If you can make straight cuts, follow a plan, and use basic tools (miter saw, drill, sander), you're qualified. We start with beginner-friendly projects and scale up from there. You don't need decades of experience—you just need to be willing to follow the process.

I don't have a fancy workshop. Will this still work? +

Yes. One of our top earners works out of a one-car garage with a miter saw, sander, and drill. Another builds in his basement. You don't need a $50k workshop. The projects we teach are designed to be built with basic tools in small spaces. If you have a place to work and a few essential tools, you're good to go.

What if I already sell a few things but can't make it consistent? +

Then you're in the right place. The difference between "I sold something once" and "I make $3K/month consistently" is having a system. We'll show you how to turn random one-off sales into predictable monthly income by stacking platforms, optimizing pricing, and building a product line that sells on repeat.

How is this different from just watching YouTube videos? +

YouTube teaches you how to build. We teach you what to build, how to price it, where to sell it, and how to turn it into consistent income. YouTube is great for technique. But it won't tell you which projects are actually profitable, how to write a listing that converts, or how to handle your first customer message. That's what we do.

Do I need expensive tools? +

Nope. The "starter projects" we recommend can be built with: a miter saw, a drill, a sander, and basic hand tools. As you make sales and reinvest, you can upgrade. But you don't need a table saw, planer, or jointer to get started. We'll show you exactly what tools you need for each project tier.

How long until I make my first sale? +

Most members make their first sale within 30 days. Some within the first week. It depends on how fast you build, list, and execute.
