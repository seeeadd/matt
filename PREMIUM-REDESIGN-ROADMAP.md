1. **Visual Hierarchy**
   - Clear primary action

   - Eye naturally flows down page
   - Nothing feels "hidden"

2. **Strategic White Space**
   - Elements breathe

   - Creates focus on important elements
   - Premium feel

3. **Depth & Layering**
   - Cards feel interactive
   - Shadows create dimension
   - Parallax adds engagement
   - Professional 3D feel

4. **Contrast & Focal Points**
   - CTAs pop off page
   - Headlines grab attention
   - Important info highlighted
   - Clear reading path

5. **Typography that Commands**
   - Headlines demand attention
   - Body text easy to scan
   - Strategic bold/color
   - Proper hierarchy

6. **Color Psychology**
   - Orange/Gold: Action, urgency
   - Dark tones: Masculinity, trust
   - Green: Success, money
   - White: Clean, premium

7. **Micro-Interactions**
   - Hover states feel responsive
   - Clicks have feedback
   - Animations add delight
   - Page feels alive
